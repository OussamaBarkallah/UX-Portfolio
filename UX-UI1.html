Orange Design System

Overview

During my internship at Orange XDLab, I contributed to the evolution of the Orange Unified Design System (ODS), a large-scale design ecosystem created to ensure consistent, scalable, and accessible digital experiences across Orange products and platforms. I collaborated closely with senior designers, the Product Owner, and developers on the maintenance, improvement, and expansion of the design system through reusable components, documentation, icon systems, illustration libraries, and collaborative workflows.

Context

    As Orange’s digital ecosystem expanded across multiple products and teams, maintaining consistency, scalability, and efficient collaboration became increasingly important. The Orange Unified Design System aimed to:
  • Standardize UI patterns and reusable components.
  • Improve collaboration between design and development teams.
  • Accelerate product workflows.
  • Ensure accessibility and usability consistency.

Challenges

    Key challenges included:
  • Maintaining visual consistency across multiple products and interfaces.
  • Improving outdated or poorly structured UI components.
  • Ensuring scalability and maintainability of reusable assets.
  • Organizing and optimizing large icon libraries for better discoverability
  • Supporting efficient collaboration between designers, developers, and product teams.
  • Creating reusable promotional and illustration systems adaptable to different contexts.
  • Aligning Orange’s visual identity with modern design system standards and user experience expectations.

Key Contributions :

  • Contributed to a large-scale design system composed of more than 60 reusable components and 10 UI kits delivered in Figma, following design directions established in Sketch and collaborative workflows through FigJam
  • Improved and redesigned several poorly structured components to enhance usability, visual consistency, and scalability across product experiences
  • Collaborated closely with the Product Owner and development teams to ensure system consistency, component maintainability, and technical feasibility
  • Designed a reusable Promotions Kit (banners, hero sections) to harmonize marketing content and product communication across multiple platforms
  • Refactored and optimized the Solaris icon library by cleaning structures, fixing inconsistencies, removing duplicates, and improving organization through metadata and categorization
  • Created a modular 2D illustration and character system aligned with Orange’s visual identity to ensure flexible and consistent use across multiple interfaces
  • Conducted in-depth benchmarks of leading international design systems (Material, Atlassian, Carbon, etc.) and formulated strategic recommendations that contributed to the evolution of the Orange Unified Design System
  • Structured a version-based changelog system to improve update tracking, transparency, and collaboration between teams
  • Participated in multi-site onboarding workshops across France and created documentation and collaborative resources dedicated to onboarding new team members
